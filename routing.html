Applied AI, routing

What one extra alert bought

The rules from the repository run in your browser. Send them a message in English, Roman Urdu or Urdu and see which queue it lands in.

Baseline: 3 of 10 review-worthy messages reached review. Revised: 9 of 10, with one unnecessary review.

Evidence

  • Counts for each language
  • Inspect the cases
  • Routing rules
  • Consent and regression tests

3 of 10 reached review

A synthetic support desk needs to route account compromise and unexpected charges to a reviewer. Three English phrases caught only 3 of 10 review-worthy messages across English, Roman Urdu and Urdu script.

9 of 10 reached review

Adding six phrases and normalizing punctuation and Urdu characters caught six more cases. One ordinary message also reached review: it contained a matching phrase and then negated it. An English paraphrase still went undetected.

The fixture set has 18 items. Its size makes every mistake inspectable and limits how far the rates generalize. Separate tests check that a handoff can include a transcript only with explicit consent.
