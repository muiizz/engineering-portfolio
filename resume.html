Current project

Founder, Divia

2026 to present

A doctor-led digital coaching programme for people with diabetes and prediabetes in Pakistan. Patients talk to an AI coach on WhatsApp under physician oversight. Live, with its first four patients sending readings every day.

  • Designed the safety architecture: a deterministic rules engine runs before the language model and decides thresholds, emergency words, double-dose detection and urgent routing. The model is the second safety net, never the first.
  • Every threshold is tested just below, at and just above its value, in both mg/dL and mmol/L. The rules engine is held at 100% branch coverage, with scheduled mutation testing, and each test is traced to an entry in a hazard log.
  • Glucose readings are taken from photos of the patient’s meter: two independent reads must agree before a value is accepted, and a plausible low value is escalated as urgent at once.
  • Built an automated evaluation runner that checks the coach’s behaviour against a clinical test workbook, which is the source of truth for expected behaviour.
  • Clinical governance: all clinical content and thresholds are versioned and approved by the Chief Medical Officer; every outbound message records the content versions and the model used.
  • Data protection: the model receives a patient code, never a name or phone number; row-level security on every patient table; health data exchanged as HL7 FHIR R4 with LOINC and SNOMED CT codes.
  • Technologies: Python (FastAPI, Pydantic, pytest, mypy), PostgreSQL, TypeScript (Next.js), Docker, WhatsApp Cloud API.
